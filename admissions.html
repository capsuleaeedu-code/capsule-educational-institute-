Start Your Application Journey

Capsule Educational Institute offers guidance and admissions support for learners interested in higher education and professional training.

Our team can help you enquire about programme options, understand admission requirements and learn about the next steps in the process.

Admission decisions, eligibility requirements, programme availability and final fees are subject to confirmation by the relevant institution or course provider.

How the Process Works

1. Choose a Programme

Explore our programme information and identify the course or study pathway you would like to enquire about.

2. Contact Our Team

Send us your enquiry by phone, email or WhatsApp. Tell us which programme you are interested in.

3. Discuss Requirements

Ask about eligibility, required documents, study format, schedules, fees and the application process.

4. Follow the Application Steps

Our team will explain the relevant next steps once the programme details and requirements have been confirmed.

Documents and Requirements

Required documents vary according to the programme and its provider. Before submitting an application, contact our team to confirm the current requirements.

You may be asked to provide academic records, identification or other supporting documents, depending on the programme.

Please do not send sensitive personal documents through an unsecured channel. Ask our team about the appropriate submission method before sharing them.

Higher Education Enquiries

We provide guidance and admissions support for learners exploring undergraduate and postgraduate study options, including programmes associated with Rabindranath Tagore University (RNTU).

Programme availability, recognition, eligibility, study mode, fees and admission decisions should be confirmed with the relevant institution before making an application.
